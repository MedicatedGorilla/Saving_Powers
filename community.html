A resident's guide

How to make your voice count.

You don't need to be a land-use expert. Start with the public record and follow the participation instructions for each case.

01

Find the proposal

Look up the address, application number, and review type on the Planning Projects page.

02

Read the notice

Look for hearing dates, comment deadlines, staff findings, and the official way to submit written or spoken testimony.

03

Contact the right office

Ask Planning staff about specific applications. Reach out to City Council about broader city policy and legislative decisions.

Important: Writing to elected officials may not count as testimony in a particular land-use proceeding. Follow the official notice if your comments need to enter its record.
