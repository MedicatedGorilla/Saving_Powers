Featured campaign · NW Powers Avenue

The Northside Church land-use case.

Benton County voted 2–1 on October 20, 2026, to adopt its approval order for the Northside Church proposal. Neighbors have raised concerns about its scale, road safety, and compatibility with surrounding rural homes.

Our purpose · Beyond Powers Avenue

Stronger neighborhoods. Better decisions.

Saving Powers began around one proposal. The questions it raised matter throughout Benton County.

Residents deserve access to the facts behind land-use proposals, fair application of local standards, and real opportunities to participate before decisions shape their neighborhoods for decades.

What guides our work

Respect for neighborhoods. Clear information. Consistent land-use standards.

Each proposal has its own facts and review process. Our goal is informed community participation—not opposition to development simply because it's new.
