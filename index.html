Why the Alliance exists

Local decisions deserve local voices.

A development can shape a neighborhood for decades. We believe residents deserve transparent information, consistent standards, and real opportunities to participate.

Saving Powers began with a specific proposal on Powers Avenue. The Alliance aims to help neighbors understand and engage with land-use decisions across the community.
