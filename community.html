Saving Powers Alliance · Beyond Powers Avenue

Keeping our neighbors informed across Benton County.

Saving Powers began with the Northside Church proposal, but thoughtful land-use decisions matter throughout our region. The Alliance is expanding its focus on community information, transparent review, and opportunities for residents to participate.

The Northside campaign remains our immediate focus.

We're also sharing public meeting information and planning resources that may matter to neighbors in Albany and the surrounding area. Each proposal has its own process and record.

News You Need to Be Aware Of

Nov 9 · 5:15 PM Albany Planning Commission meeting

The Albany Planning Commission meets Monday, November 9, 2026, at 5:15 PM in the City Hall Council Chambers, 333 Broadalbin Street SW, with virtual participation available.

The posted agenda includes a proposed annexation and zoning-map amendment near Clover Ridge Road NE and a separate public right-of-way vacation. Knox Butte is not listed as a hearing topic in that agenda. Please check the official materials before attending or submitting testimony.

To submit written comments or request to speak virtually, email cdaa@albanyoregon.gov with your name before noon on meeting day, as directed by the agenda.

Policy discussion Data centers and local land-use rules

Albany's Planning Commission has discussed how the City might approach data-center development and the role of local land-use standards. The August 17 commission minutes record discussion of bringing the issue to City Council; they do not establish that new rules have been adopted.

Follow City Council and Planning Commission meeting materials for any formal proposals, hearings, or opportunities to comment.
