02Show your support

Put a sign of support in your yard.

A $50 donation gets you a free Saving Powers yard sign.

  1. Donate $50 or more on GoFundMe. Use your name rather than donating anonymously so the organizers can match your donation.
  2. Email savingpowers@gmail.com. Include the name used on GoFundMe to request your sign and a donation receipt.

If you've already donated $50 or more in total, email the group to request your sign and receipt.
