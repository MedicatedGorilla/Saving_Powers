What changed from the original proposal?

The original two-phase concept included an 18,902-square-foot gymnasium in Phase 1 and a much larger main sanctuary in Phase 2. Phase 2 and the parking variance were later withdrawn. During the August open-record period, the applicant clarified that the remaining building's gross floor area was 28,557 square feet, rather than the earlier 18,902-square-foot first-floor figure.
