Albany Planning Commission

The Commission will meet at City Hall, Council Chambers, 333 Broadalbin Street SW, with a virtual participation option.

The posted agenda includes an annexation/zone-map amendment hearing and a city-initiated right-of-way vacation. Check the agenda rather than assuming a particular property is being heard.

Written comments and requests to speak virtually must be emailed to cdaa@albanyoregon.gov by noon on meeting day, according to the agenda.
