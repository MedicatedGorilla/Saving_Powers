Monday, November 9, 2026 · 5:15 p.m.

Albany Planning Commission

The Commission is scheduled to meet at City Hall, Council Chambers, 333 Broadalbin Street SW, with a virtual participation option.

The posted agenda includes an annexation/zone-map amendment hearing and a city-initiated right-of-way vacation. Please use the actual agenda to confirm which locations and topics are under consideration.

According to the agenda, written comments and virtual speaking requests should be emailed to cdaa@albanyoregon.gov by noon on meeting day.
