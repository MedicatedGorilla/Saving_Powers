Show your support · Yard signs

A $50 donation gets you a free yard sign.

Support the Saving Powers appeal and help make our neighborhood's voice visible.

  1. Donate $50 or more on GoFundMe. Use your name rather than donating anonymously so we can match your donation.
  2. Email savingpowers@gmail.com. Include the name used on GoFundMe to claim your sign and request a receipt for your tax-deductible donation.

Help tell the story

Photos, video, and first-hand observations are useful too.

Neighbors can help by gathering their own photos or short videos of the area, public events, meetings, yard signs, and changing conditions—whether that's a sunset, a rainy day, or wildlife around the neighborhood. Send only material you're comfortable sharing and have permission to use.
